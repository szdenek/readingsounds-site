The author of Reading Sounds, Sean Zdenek, is a U.S.-based scholar who studies and publishes in the areas of Web accessibility, deaf studies, and visual rhetoric.   • Borrows an appropriate amount of material from the original, only what is sufficient (and no more) to put the example under critical, scholarly scrutiny;
